# Christmas Sale Photo Generation Prompt

## Complete AI Image Generation Prompt

### Primary Prompt (For DALL-E, Midjourney, Stable Diffusion, etc.)

```
Professional product photography, luxury beauty brand aesthetic, Christmas holiday sale campaign, elegant minimalist composition, studio lighting setup, premium lifestyle photography style.

SCENE COMPOSITION:
- Four CURLEA DreamCurl™ heatless hair curling rod sets arranged in an elegant, architectural composition
- Products displayed: DreamCurl Original Full Set (Mulberry color), DreamCurl Midi Full Set (Purple), DreamCurl Jumbo Full Set (Latte), and Zero Heat Mini Full Set (Olive)
- Products arranged in a sophisticated geometric layout - two sets prominently displayed in foreground, one slightly behind, creating visual depth
- The third set (free item) subtly highlighted with soft golden Christmas light or a delicate gift ribbon accent

VISUAL STYLE:
- Clean, minimalist background in soft cream/ivory or elegant deep burgundy red
- Subtle Christmas elements: delicate gold snowflakes floating in background, soft bokeh Christmas lights creating depth
- Premium fabric textures: velvet, satin, or silk drapery in Christmas colors (deep red, forest green, gold)
- Architectural lines and geometric shapes creating visual interest without overwhelming the products
- Professional studio lighting: soft, diffused key light from upper left, subtle rim lighting, warm golden hour tones

PRODUCT PRESENTATION:
- Each heatless curling rod set shown in its full glory, showcasing the premium vegan Peau de Soie fabric
- Products displayed at slight angles to show dimension and texture
- Color variety visible: rich mulberry purple, elegant latte beige, vibrant olive green, royal purple
- Products appear luxurious, high-quality, and gift-worthy
- Clean, uncluttered presentation emphasizing the premium nature of the products

CHRISTMAS ELEMENTS (Subtle & Elegant):
- Delicate gold ribbon or bow accent on the "free" third item
- Soft, out-of-focus Christmas tree lights creating bokeh in background
- Subtle snowflake patterns or frost textures (very light, not overwhelming)
- Warm golden lighting suggesting holiday warmth
- Elegant typography space reserved for "Buy 2, Get 3rd FREE" text overlay

COLOR PALETTE:
- Primary: Deep burgundy red (#8B0000), Forest green (#0F5132), Gold (#D4AF37), Cream/Ivory (#FFFEF0)
- Accent: Rich mulberry purple, elegant latte beige, vibrant olive green
- Background: Soft neutral tones (cream, ivory, or deep burgundy) that complement product colors
- Lighting: Warm golden tones, soft diffused light, subtle rim lighting

MOOD & ATMOSPHERE:
- Luxurious and premium feel
- Festive but sophisticated (not overly commercial)
- Elegant gift-giving atmosphere
- Professional beauty brand aesthetic
- Warm, inviting Christmas spirit without being kitschy
- High-end lifestyle magazine quality

TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS:
- Professional product photography quality
- Sharp focus on products, soft focus on background elements
- High resolution, commercial photography standard
- Aspect ratio: 16:9 or 4:3 for versatile use
- Depth of field: Products in sharp focus, background elements softly blurred
- Color grading: Warm, slightly desaturated for elegance, with rich product colors popping

COMPOSITION RULES:
- Rule of thirds applied
- Visual hierarchy: Two main products prominent, third product (free) subtly highlighted
- Negative space for text overlay placement
- Balanced composition with visual weight distributed evenly
- Leading lines drawing eye to the offer message area

AVOID:
- Cluttered or busy backgrounds
- Overly commercial or salesy appearance
- Cheap-looking decorations
- Harsh lighting or shadows
- Products looking like stock photos
- Too many Christmas elements competing with products
- Low-quality or amateur photography style
```

## Usage Instructions

### For AI Image Generators:

1. **DALL-E 3 (ChatGPT)**: Use the primary prompt as-is, or break into shorter sections if needed
2. **Midjourney**: Use the primary prompt, add `--ar 16:9 --style raw --v 6` for best results
3. **Stable Diffusion**: Use the primary prompt, add negative prompts: "low quality, blurry, amateur, cluttered, cheap"
4. **Adobe Firefly**: Use the primary prompt, select "Product Photography" style

### For Professional Photographers:

1. Use the composition guidelines and product arrangement suggestions
2. Follow the color palette and lighting specifications
3. Ensure products are the hero elements with Christmas as subtle accent
4. Reserve space for text overlay in upper or lower third
5. Maintain premium, luxurious aesthetic throughout

### Post-Processing Recommendations:

1. **Color Grading**: Warm, slightly desaturated tones with rich product colors
2. **Sharpening**: Enhance product details while keeping background soft
3. **Text Overlay**: Use elegant serif font (Cormorant Garamond or similar) in gold or deep burgundy
4. **Brand Consistency**: Ensure colors match CURLEA brand guidelines
5. **Optimization**: Export in multiple sizes for web, social media, and print use
