🧹 Clear Service Worker Cache

ℹ️ Why am I here?
Your browser cached old CSS/JS files with wrong MIME types. This page will clear them.
Manual Steps (if buttons don't work):
  1. Press F12 to open Developer Tools
  2. Go to the Application tab
  3. In the left sidebar, click Service Workers
  4. Click Unregister next to the service worker
  5. Click Clear storage in the left sidebar
  6. Click Clear site data
  7. Hard refresh: Ctrl+Shift+R (Windows) or Cmd+Shift+R (Mac)
